## ✨ Why BrewDesk?

Most POS systems lock you in with setup fees, hefty commissions, and hardware bundles you don't need. BrewDesk flips that model:

- 💸 **Start free — no credit card required.** The Starter plan is $0 forever.
- 📶 **Works offline, always.** Orders save locally first. Nothing breaks when Wi-Fi does.
- 💳 **Your Stripe, your money.** Payments go straight to your bank account. BrewDesk never holds your funds.
- 📈 **The more you earn, the less you pay.** Hit $15K/mo in card sales and your Pro subscription drops 50%. Hit $30K on Business and it's free.

## 🚀 Features

### Core POS
- Full order management — dine-in tables, counter, takeout
- Cash & card payments (Stripe Connect — direct to your account)
- Split payments, partial refunds, same-day full refunds
- Customisable menus with categories, add-ons, and modifiers
- Receipt printing (ESC/POS thermal) + digital receipts via Email/SMS

### Sell Smarter
- 🧠 **AI Menu Builder** — generate a full menu from your business type in seconds
- 📱 **QR Ordering** — customers scan, order & pay from their phone, no app needed
- 🎟️ **Loyalty Stamps** — digital stamp cards, auto-earned at checkout
- 👥 **CRM** — customer profiles built automatically from visits; no manual entry
- 💬 **Messaging** — Email, SMS & WhatsApp receipts, waitlist notifications, reward alerts

### Run Your Business
- 📊 **Analytics** — daily sales, top items, staff performance, payment breakdowns
- 🗂️ **Staff Management** — roles, PIN login, time clock, attendance log
- 🪑 **Table Management** — floor plan, waitlist, reservations
- 🖥️ **Customer Display** — second screen shows order in real time
- 🖨️ **Kitchen Printing** — auto-print tickets to kitchen printers
- 📋 **Z-Reports** — one-tap end-of-day closing with full sales summary
- ☁️ **Google Drive Backup** — automated daily backup of your data

### Built for the Real World
- ✅ **Offline-first** — full functionality without internet; syncs when back online
- 🌍 **8 Languages** — English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay
- 🎨 **Custom Brand Colours** — match your café's identity across the POS and customer display
- 📲 **PWA** — install directly from the browser on any tablet or desktop (no app store needed)
- 🔒 **Secure** — bcrypt PIN hashing, device token auth, strict multi-tenant isolation

## ⚡ Get Started in 5 Minutes
