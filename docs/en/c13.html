13Device modes & customer display

When you open BrewDesk on a new device, it asks: "How will you use this device?" You pick one job for it. Here are all the choices:

  1. Main POS — your main till. This is your primary register.
  2. Sub POS — an extra till to help out. (Business plan.)
  3. Staff Ordering — your staff take orders from any tablet or phone on your network, and the orders go straight to the main till. (Pro plan; it shows how many "slots used".)
  4. Customer Display — a second screen that shows customers their order.
  5. Check-in — a self-check-in screen for walk-in guests.
  6. Order Kiosk — coming soon.
Where to turn it on

Staff Ordering and Customer Display are turned on in Settings › Add-ons (Devices section) — Staff Ordering is a Pro add-on. Then, on the other device, open it and pick that mode from "How will you use this device?".

Staff Ordering

Staff Ordering lets your team take orders from an extra tablet or phone. The orders fly straight to the main till. Here's how to set it up.

  1. Turn on Staff Ordering in Settings › Add-ons (Devices section). It's a Pro add-on.
  2. Open BrewDesk on the extra tablet or phone.
  3. When it asks "How will you use this device?", choose Staff Ordering.
  4. Your staff sign in and start taking orders.
  5. Each order flows to the main till by itself. (This device has no payment screen and no owner settings — it's just for taking orders.)
Great for busy times: staff can take orders right at the table, or walk down a long line so no one waits.
Orders always go through your main till. A Staff Ordering tablet hands every order to the main till over your shop Wi-Fi — so it keeps working even when the internet is out, as long as the main till is switched on and on the same network.
If it can't reach the main till you'll see "Not sent to the kitchen" — "Could not reach the Main POS. This order has not reached the kitchen yet." Two buttons: "Try again" sends it once more (the same order can never go in twice), or "Entered on Main POS" if you gave up and typed it into the main till yourself. Only tap that second one if you really did — otherwise BrewDesk sends the order again later and it lands twice.

Customer display

A customer display is a second screen that faces the customer. It shows them their order and the price as you add things. Here's how to set one up.

Where to turn it on

Turn it on in Settings › Add-ons › Customer Display, then on the spare device pick the Customer Display mode.

  1. Find a spare tablet or screen and open BrewDesk on it.
  2. When it asks what this device is for, choose Customer display.
  3. The screen joins up with your till by itself. No login needed.
  4. Now point the screen toward the customer. As you add items at the till, they show up on this screen too.
  5. The Pro plan lets you use 1 display. The Business plan lets you use up to 3.
Good to know: a customer display only shows things. It can't take orders or send anything — it just shows the order to your customer.
