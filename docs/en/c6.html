6Reservations & appointments

A reservation is when a customer books a table or a time before they come in. You can keep all your bookings in one place. Let's add one.

Where to turn it on

Turn Reservations on in Settings › Add-ons › Reservations (flip the switch). It needs Tables on first — if Tables is off, a pop-up offers "Enable Both," just tap it. Booking salon or spa Appointments is a Pro add-on in the same place.

Before you begin: set your opening hours in Settings › Hours & shifts. Reservations only offer times that fall inside your open shifts — so if a time isn't showing, check your hours first.

  1. Open the reservations page. From the Table Map, tap "Reservations" (top right) — or open the Scheduler.
  2. Tap the day you want on the calendar.
  3. Tap "New Reservation". A form opens.
  4. Fill in the Guest name, Phone, Party size (how many people), Date, and Time.
  5. Optional: pick a Table and a Staff member, and add a Note. For a salon or spa, picking the staff member is the main thing.
  6. Tap Save. The guest automatically gets a confirmation that says "you're booked" — by email, SMS, or WhatsApp, depending on what you've set up.
  7. When a booked guest arrives, open their reservation card and tap "Check In". It turns to "Seated" (and gives them a table if needed).
  8. Someone walks in with no booking? Tap the "Walk-in" option — it seats the guest right away.
Seated bookings close themselves. Once a reservation is "Seated", it moves into past bookings on its own when that table gets paid, when the order is cancelled, or when you close out the day. No more yesterday's guests sitting in your reservation list.
Guests who never turned up — mark them all at closing. Switch on "Auto No-Show on Close" in Settings › Reservations › Settings, under Booking Policy & Guest Portal. Then, as the description says: "closing the day automatically marks any still-Confirmed reservations from that day as No-Show, and sends each guest a notice email explaining why." It's off until you turn it on. Only bookings still sitting at Confirmed are touched — seated, completed and cancelled ones are left exactly as they are, and re-closing the same day won't email anyone twice.

The guest's email is matter-of-fact, not a telling-off: the subject is "We couldn't confirm your visit", it says the table was held but the arrival couldn't be confirmed, and it invites them to contact you if that's wrong.

Scheduler vs Reservations: the Scheduler is for salon/spa appointments (booked by staff member and time). Table reservations are for dining (a party at a table). They share the same calendar area, so all your bookings live in one place.
No internet? Check-ins still work. With the internet down you can still tap "Check In," "No Show" or Cancel — the change shows on screen straight away and uploads by itself when you're back online. Only making or editing a booking is blocked, and the screen tells you why: "Cannot create a reservation while offline — the booking calendar is also written from outside the store, so it could double-book. Reconnect and try again." If the list can't load at all you'll see "Showing the last list received," and the bookings you already had stay on screen.
Show this off

Bookings come built in. You don't need a second app or an extra fee. Restaurants book tables; salons and spas book staff time — all on the same calendar.

Waitlist (line for tables)

A waitlist is the line of guests waiting for a free table. BrewDesk holds the line for you, and texts a guest the moment their table is ready — so they don't have to crowd your door.

Where to turn it on

Turn it on in Settings › Add-ons › Waitlist (flip the switch). It needs Tables on first — tap "Enable Both" if a pop-up asks.

  1. Open the Waitlist. From the Table Map, tap "Waitlist" (top right).
  2. Tap "Add Guest". A small form opens.
  3. Enter the guest's Name, Party size (how many people), and Phone (so they can be texted). Add a note if you like, then save.
  4. When a table frees up, find that guest and tap "Notify". They get a "your table is ready" text (SMS or WhatsApp).
  5. Then seat them — and you're done.
Why add a phone number? The phone number is how the guest gets the "table ready" text. Without it, you'd have to call their name the old-fashioned way.
The line keeps moving with no internet. Adding a guest, seating, No Show and Cancel all work offline. You'll see "Saved on this device" and it uploads on its own when the connection returns — a guest added offline gets their ticket number after it uploads. Refreshing the screen won't lose the line either: it's kept on the device, and you'll see "Showing the last list received." The one thing that needs internet is "Notify" — the "your table is ready" text can't go out until you're back online, so call that guest by name for now.
No tables? Call the guest and start their order. If your shop runs as a counter (no table map), the button on the guest's card says "Start Order" instead of "Seat." Tap it and BrewDesk marks them seated and drops you straight into the order screen for that guest. If something is already in the basket it asks first — "Order in progress" → tap "Hold & start" and the old basket is parked in your held orders, so nothing is lost.
Your kiosk wears your name. On a tablet set to Waitlist Kiosk, the top of the screen now shows your shop logo and shop name — it uses the picture you already saved in Settings › Store info, so there is nothing extra to upload. The phone box also opens on your country's dialling code (+1, +82, +66 …), taken from your shop's country, so guests just type their number.
