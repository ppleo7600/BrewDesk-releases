1Welcome & overview

BrewDesk is a till for your shop. It takes orders and money for cafes, restaurants, bars, bakeries, and salons. It works on a laptop or a tablet you already have. It even keeps working when the internet stops. This page is a quick look around. It takes about one minute to read.

Is this for you? If you sell things and take money, BrewDesk is for you. You do not need to be good with computers. You do not need a tech helper.
