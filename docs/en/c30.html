7Set up card payments · Connect Stripe

To take card payments and online-order payments, connect a Stripe account in your business's name to BrewDesk once. It usually takes about 15 minutes, or 1–2 business days if Stripe reviews documents. Payments go straight to your Stripe account and never pass through BrewDesk.

Where

Open Settings › Payments and scroll down to the Stripe Connect card and turn on its switch (before connecting it reads Card Payments Locked). The card has no separate connect button; the switch is the button. You need to be signed in as the Owner.

The Stripe account is yours. Even if a reseller helped set up your store, connect Stripe with your own details and the store's bank account. Once connected, you have your own Stripe Dashboard login, and the account that receives the money owns the Stripe account.

Check your country first

Based on the 13 countries where BrewDesk is sold. In some countries card readers (Stripe Terminal) work as well; in others only online and QR card payments work; in some nothing works.

For stores in unsupported countries, take cards on your own separate card terminal and record them in the POS with the record-only Card payment method. Do not connect Stripe Connect in that case.

Have these ready

Match these before connecting — the country cannot be changed later

The Stripe account's country is fixed when you first connect, from the country set in your store info. Do not change the store's country after connecting. If the country is wrong, you have to disconnect (Disconnect) and connect again, so before connecting, check Country, the address and Store Name on Settings › Store Info. The store name is passed to Stripe as the display name.

Stripe verifies your business and its representative. With these at hand you can finish in one go.

What Stripe asks for
☐ Business details — business registration number (Singapore UEN · US EIN · Australia ABN · Malaysia SSM number, etc.), registered business name and address, business type (individual or company — the same as the registry record)
☐ Industry and store description, plus a website or social media address (a store description works if there is none)
☐ Name shown on card statements — the store name customers will see on their card statement
☐ Identity of the representative — photo ID (+ selfie), date of birth, home address. In Singapore, Singpass is the fastest. Without Singpass, use an NRIC, FIN (Work Pass) or passport plus a selfie; if the representative lives outside Singapore, only a passport is accepted.
☐ For a company, details of owners with 25% or more and of directors
☐ Payout bank account — in the store's (business's) name, at a bank in that country
☐ Your own email (one you read every day — payout, dispute and document-request emails go there) and your phone in hand (verification codes, two-step verification)
Singapore: the person listed as Owner or Director on ACRA BizFile must enter the details themselves; only then does it finish without a Letter of Authorization. If staff or anyone else fills it in, Stripe asks for a separate Letter of Authorization, so it is quickest when the owner does it.
Do not
① Create the Stripe account with another company's or another person's details or email — the payouts and the account ownership would become theirs.
② Change the store's country after connecting.
③ Turn the connection switch off and press Disconnect just to try it — card payments stop immediately.

Steps

  1. In BrewDesk, open Settings › Payments and turn on the switch on the Stripe Connect card further down. Stripe's sign-up page opens.
  1. Check that Email address is your own email and press Continue. If you already have a Stripe account, sign in to it; if not, create one there. Enter the verification codes sent by email or phone, and solve the bot check (picking pictures) if it appears.
  1. Enter the business details — business type, registration number, business name and address, industry (restaurant or café), website (or a store description), and the name shown on card statements. Then verify the representative (photo ID; in Singapore tap Singpass or upload an ID photo and a selfie) and, for a company, enter the owners' and directors' details.
  1. Enter the payout bank account. It must be a bank account in the store's (business's) name, in that country.
  1. Review and submit. You return to BrewDesk's Settings screen automatically.
  2. If the window was closed halfway or Stripe ended the session, you land back on Settings › Payments. If the card still reads Card Payments Locked, it is not finished. Turn the switch on again and it resumes from where it stopped, on the same Stripe account.
When the connection succeeds, a Stripe connected notice is emailed once to the store email. Once connected, the record-only Card (Record Only) payment method becomes inactive as "Superseded by Stripe" — Stripe takes priority; this is not a fault.

Reading the connection status

  • When the switch is on, Charges shows Enabled and Onboarding shows Complete, you are done. Card payments and online-order card payments can be taken. In countries where card readers work, go on to connect a reader by following Card terminals.
  • If the switch is off and the card reads Card Payments Locked, the connection is unfinished or documents are under review. If Stripe needs more documents, what is needed is shown in Stripe's email or in your Stripe Dashboard. Check your inbox, submit the documents, then turn the switch on again to continue. It usually takes a few minutes, or 1–2 business days if review is needed.

Who charges what

  • Stripe's processing fee is charged by Stripe directly to your Stripe account. For Singapore domestic cards it is 3.4% + S$0.50 (international cards cost more).
  • BrewDesk's fee is 0% on in-store payments and 0.4% on online orders only.
  • To cover online-order costs in your menu price, use Online price increase in QR ordering. It raises online menu prices by a percentage and leaves in-store and table QR prices unchanged. For an average order of S$8–20 we suggest 10%.

After connecting

  • To use a card reader, follow Card terminals: Discover Readers → Connect.
  • For taking payments, see Getting paid.

Do not disconnect just to try it

Turning the switch off on a connected card opens a Disconnect Stripe? confirmation. Pressing Disconnect there deletes the Stripe connected account and stops card payments immediately (cash keeps working). Only the Owner can do this. Do it only when the country was wrong and the account must be connected again, and outside business hours. You can reconnect any time.
