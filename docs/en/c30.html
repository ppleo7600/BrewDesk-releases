7Card payment setup · Connect Stripe

To take card, QR and online-order payments, connect a Stripe account in your store's name to BrewDesk once. Filling it in usually takes 15 minutes; if Stripe needs to review, allow 1–2 business days. Money goes straight to your Stripe account, never through BrewDesk.

4 steps at a glance

① Gather your documents → ② On the POS, open Settings › Payments and tap Stripe under Payment providers at the bottom → ③ Tap Connect Stripe and fill in Stripe's pages yourself → ④ Come back and check it says Connected

The Stripe account belongs to you, the owner. Even if a reseller (distributor) installed BrewDesk for you, use your own details and your store's bank account. Do it on the Main POS while signed in as the Owner. It can't be done from the owner app on your phone.

① What to have ready (Singapore)

First check your store country and name — the country can't change after connecting

In Settings › Store Info, check that Country, address and Store Name are correct. The Stripe account's country is fixed from this value when you first connect.

ItemNote
☐ UENStripe looks up your ACRA record with the UEN. Enter the business name, address and business type exactly as on ACRA BizFile.
☐ The representative · SingpassThe person listed on ACRA as Owner or Director fills it in. Singpass is the fastest way to verify. Without Singpass: NRIC, FIN or passport (colour, original photo) plus a selfie. A representative living outside Singapore must use a passport.
☐ Payout bank accountA Singapore bank account in the business's name.
☐ Companies: owners and directorsAnyone owning 25% or more, and the directors. Stripe pre-fills them from ACRA — just confirm.
☐ Your email and phoneAn email you read daily (payouts and document requests arrive there) and your phone for verification codes.
☐ Store description · statement nameWebsite or social link (or a short description) and the name that appears on customers' card statements.

Source (Stripe): Singapore verification requirements · Private companies · Accepted documents · checked 2026-10

If someone other than the representative fills it in, Stripe asks for a Letter of Authority signed by the CEO or a Director (dated within 12 months). It's fastest if the owner does it.

②③ Connect

  1. On the Main POS, open Settings › Payments and tap the Stripe row under Payment providers at the bottom.
  1. Tap Connect Stripe. Stripe's sign-up page opens.
  1. Check the Email address is yours and tap Continue. Enter the codes sent to your phone and email.
  2. Fill in what Stripe asks, in order — UEN and business details → representative verification (Singpass) → for companies, confirm owners and directors → payout account.
  3. Submit on the last page. You return to BrewDesk's Payments screen automatically.

④ Check it's done

  • When the Stripe row says Connected, you're done. A Stripe connected email is sent to your store email once. Charges here means "able to take payments" (not fees).
  • Still Not connected? It isn't finished yet, or Stripe is reviewing. See If you get stuck.
  • Once connected, Stripe card in Payment methods and the Needs connection cells in the table unlock. Counter terminal · record only (your own card terminal) keeps working alongside it.

Stores in unsupported countries take cards on their own card terminal and record them at the POS as Counter terminal · record only.

Who charges what

  • Stripe's processing fee is taken by Stripe from your Stripe account. For domestic cards in Singapore it is 3.4% + S$0.50 (international cards cost more). Check the exact rates in your Stripe Dashboard.
  • BrewDesk's fee is 0% on in-store payments and 0.4% on online orders only.
  • To cover the online-order fee in your prices, use Online price increase in QR ordering.

After connecting

  • To use a card reader, connect it by following Card terminal.
  • Choose which payments you take where in the Payment methods and "Where you accept" table on the same Payments screen. For taking payments, see Taking payments.

Don't disconnect to "test"

Tapping Disconnect at the bottom of the Stripe window and confirming deletes the Stripe connection and stops Stripe card and QR payments immediately (cash and record-only cards keep working). The owner app gets a payment alert. Only do it when you must reconnect because the country was wrong, and outside opening hours.
