29Glossary

Terms that come up often in this manual and on BrewDesk screens, grouped by topic. Follow the link on each row for the full explanation.

Devices & screens

TermMeaning
Main POSThe store's anchor till — there is only one. The store's first device becomes the Main POS automatically; changing it goes through Replace Main POS and an email code. If it goes off, nothing switches over automatically. → Device modes & customer display · Multiple devices & reliability
Sub POS (extra till)An extra till that can also take payments. Registered with the owner's email sign-in and a verification code; up to 3 tills including the main one on the Business plan. → Device modes & customer display
Staff OrderingA tablet or phone that staff use to take orders only. Orders travel over the store Wi-Fi to the Main POS. Pro 3 devices, Business unlimited. → Staff Ordering
Customer DisplayA screen turned towards the customer showing the order and prices. It only displays — it can't take orders. Pro 1, Business up to 3. → Customer display
Waitlist KioskA self check-in screen where walk-in guests add themselves to the waitlist. Formerly called "Check-in". → Device modes & customer display · Waitlist
Pairing (registering a device)Tapping "Register as a new device at Main POS" in the new device's app, then choosing its job and approving on the Main POS. The job is always chosen on the Main POS. → Add a device to your main till

Selling & payments

TermMeaning
Practice mode · LiveEvery store starts in practice mode, where orders don't count as real sales. When ready, switch to live (real trading) with Go Live on the Main POS. It can't be undone. → Going live
HoldParks an unpaid order so you can reopen it later. → Hold an order
Quick discount · Free itemA one-off price cut or giveaway made at the till, recorded with a reason chip. Free items are counted separately from discounts at closing. → Quick discount or free item
Record Only paymentA way to just record a card payment taken on a separate terminal that isn't connected to BrewDesk. Shown as card-brand buttons (Visa, MasterCard and so on) or Card (External) on the payment screen. → Getting paid
Split · Split PaySplit divides one bill between several people, evenly or by item; Split Pay lets one person pay with several methods, such as cash + card. → Split the bill
Cash Payment RoundingRounds cash totals to the smallest coin actually in use. Cash only; the rounded amount shows as a Cash Rounding line on receipts and at closing. → Cash payment rounding
Add-onsExtra features switched on and off in Settings › Add-ons. Some are free, some Pro only, and messaging is pay-as-you-go. → Add-ons & extras

Closing & cash

TermMeaning
Close DayWrapping up the day and locking in its totals. You can't close while unpaid orders or an open cash drawer session remain. → Reports & closing the day
X-Report · Z-ReportX is a mid-day check; Z is the final report saved when you close the day. → Reports & closing the day
Additional ClosingA separate report ("Additional Closing #1") for payments that arrived after a day was already closed. The original Z-Report is never changed. → Late orders & missed days
Cash drawer sessionThe day's record for checking the cash in the drawer: Start Session (starting amount) → Pay In / Pay Out → END SESSION (counted amount), showing expected, counted and variance. Owner and managers only. → Cash drawer
Opening floatThe money put in the drawer when a session starts. It can be fixed with Fix opening float only until the first transaction. → Cash drawer

Staff, permissions & plans

TermMeaning
Role chipThe coloured chip in the top bar showing which role this till is acting as: Owner (orange) · Manager (blue) · Staff (grey). Tap it to switch roles and open the Time Clock. → Permissions & role chip
Time ClockThe panel where staff clock in and out with their PIN and choose who is running the till. Hours worked feed into reports. → Staff quick start · Staff & permissions
Idle LockSwitches a till left alone for a while to the Select Profile lock screen. Formerly "PIN Login"; turning it off leaves PIN sign-in and permissions working. → Staff & permissions · permissions
Owner PINThe owner's 4-digit PIN for stepping up to the owner role and approving important actions such as removing a staff member. If forgotten, reset it with a 6-digit code sent to the sign-up email. → Forgot a PIN?
Manager PINA shared PIN that lets someone do manager tasks without their own account. Only the owner can change it, and while it is still the default 0000 nobody can switch to manager. → Permissions & manager PIN
Staff PINEach staff member's own 4-digit PIN, used on the lock screen and for clocking in and out. → Add a staff member
Plans (Starter · Pro · Business)Starter is free; Pro and Business come with a one-month free trial. The plan decides which features you get and how many devices (tills, customer displays, staff-ordering devices). → Your plan & billing · device limit
Installer partnerA company that installs the store and keeps managing settings afterwards. Connected with the owner's approval; the owner can disconnect at any time. → Installer partner
