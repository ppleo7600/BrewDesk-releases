25Installation checklist

The running order for an installer setting BrewDesk up in a store and handing it over to the owner. Work from the top, finishing and ticking off one item at a time. The equipment and network you need are listed in Equipment & network.

Before you start

Do the whole install in practice mode. Orders entered while practising don't count as real sales, and the final step's "Reset and go live" wipes them before trading starts. Also make sure the owner can receive email — choosing the main POS, registering an extra till and going live all use an email verification code.

  1. ☐ Account & sign-up — sign up at brewdesk.io with an email the owner actually reads, and finish the setup wizard (business type → store info → service mode → add-ons → plan → menu → done). If the email was mistyped, fix it right away. → First-time setup · Change your login email
  2. ☐ Set the main POS — install the BrewDesk app on the counter device and sign in with the owner's email. The store's first device becomes the Main POS automatically. If another device is already the main one, switch with Replace Main POS (email verification code). → Device modes & customer display · Multiple devices & reliability
  3. ☐ Approve the other devices — for staff ordering or a customer display, first turn them on in the main POS's Settings › Add-ons. On the new device's app tap "Register as a new device at Main POS", then on the main POS popup choose the Device role and tap "Approve". Extra tills (Sub POS) register with the owner's email sign-in and a verification code. Name each device in Settings › Devices ("Counter 1" and so on). → Add a device · device limit
  4. ☐ Add printers & test print — in Settings › Printers, add the receipt printer (Main Receipt) and kitchen printer (Kitchen), and use "Test Print" to confirm paper comes out. If text is cut off or garbled, fix it with "Printing looks wrong?". Turn on the cash-drawer option if the store uses one. → Receipts & printers · printer equipment
  5. ☐ Payment methods & card reader — in Settings › Payments, connect the owner's Stripe account and turn on the methods the store uses: Cash · Card (Record Only) · Voucher. Connect a card reader with "Discover Readers" → "Connect". If the country rounds cash, set cash rounding here too. → Getting paid · card readers · cash rounding
  6. ☐ Tax & receipts — in Settings › Tax & Service, confirm the tax rate and Tax Inclusive Pricing with the owner. Design the receipt in Settings › Receipts, and for counter stores set order numbers in Settings › Tables. → Set your tax · Order & queue numbers
  7. ☐ Menu — add categories, items and options. If the store has a menu already, AI menu building or import saves time. Double-check prices and tax display with the owner. → Building your menu · Import & export
  8. ☐ Staff & PINs — for stores using staff features (Pro and up), turn on Staff in Settings › Add-ons and add each person with a role and 4-digit PIN. Set the owner PIN, and always change the manager PIN away from 0000. Go through the permissions table (refunds and so on) with the owner and decide whether to use Idle Lock. → Add a staff member · Permissions & manager PIN
  9. ☐ Test order, refund & closing — in practice mode, run it exactly like a real day: order → payment → check the receipt and kitchen ticket print → check staff-ordering orders reach the main POS and the kitchen → refund (check the approval PIN) → start and end a cash drawer session → Close Day. → Taking orders · Refunds · Cash drawer · Reports & closing the day
  10. ☐ Go live — on the main POS, tap "Go Live" at the top of the screen. Fill in the checklist (store name, address, tax rate, menu and so on), then usually choose "Reset and go live" (wipe practice records and start clean) and enter the code sent to the owner's email. Menu, tax, printer, staff and Stripe settings all stay. → Going live
Check with the owner before handing over. ① The owner can step the role up and down with their own owner PIN, ② they will be alerted if the main till goes offline, ③ selling works without the internet (Working offline), ④ show the team Staff quick start.
Looking after the store after the install? Use the installer-partner connection. It is approved through an approval email sent to the owner, and the owner can disconnect it at any time (Installer partner).
