20Add-ons & extras

Add-ons are extra features you can switch on or off. They all live in one place. You only pay for the ones you use.

Turn add-ons on and off

  1. Open Settings › Add-ons. You'll see cards grouped as Operations, Guest Experience, and Devices, each with an on/off switch (green means on).
  2. Tap a switch to turn a feature on or off. Free add-ons work on every plan; some (like Online Ordering/QR, Loyalty, Staff, and Scheduler) are Pro; SMS is pay-as-you-go.
  3. Some add-ons need another one first. Example: turning on Reservations while Tables is off shows a pop-up "Reservations requires Tables" — tap "Enable Both". Turning off something other add-ons need shows "Disable All".
  4. Next to Add-ons in the settings sidebar there's a small counter ("number on / 16") showing how many of the 16 add-ons are on.
  5. For text messages (SMS), add some money to your balance first (see Chapter 12). Then you only pay for the texts you actually send.
"Pay-as-you-go" means you pay a little as you use it, instead of a set monthly price. Top up when your balance runs low.

When a feature is locked

Staff tapped something that's locked? They can ask you, from there. On the web, tapping a locked feature (Staff Ordering, Customer Display, Waitlist Kiosk) offers to send you the instructions instead of leaving them stuck: "Want us to email the owner instructions for turning on Staff Ordering?" → "Send email". You get it in your inbox, they get on with the queue. It only sends once a day per feature — a second try says "We already sent that email today — please check your inbox."

On an iPad or the desktop app you get a short explanation instead of the email button (App Store rules) — change your plan from your BrewDesk account on the web.
