15Working offline

You don't always need the internet. If it stops, BrewDesk keeps working and saves everything. When the internet comes back, it catches up on its own. There is nothing for you to do.

  1. If the internet drops, just keep taking orders the same as always.
  2. Your orders are saved right on the device, so nothing is lost.
  3. You can still print receipts while offline.
  4. When the internet comes back, everything sends up on its own. (That means it copies your orders to the cloud and tidies up.) Nothing gets sent twice.
Show this off

Always open for business. A weak signal or an outage won't stop even one sale. Many other tills only work with the internet on — BrewDesk doesn't have that problem.

Signing in offline

Signing in while offline. With no internet, only an account that has already signed in on this device can get in. For any other account you'll see "You're offline. Only an account that has signed in on this device before can be used offline."

Pairing devices offline

You can still add devices with no internet. Open the sign-in screen in the installed BrewDesk app while offline and you'll see "Can't reach the internet — looking for your store's main POS," then it moves to the find the main till screen on its own. As long as your shop Wi-Fi is up, you can pair a new tablet without any internet (see 14.1).
Your extra devices keep working too — over the shop Wi-Fi. When the internet drops, tablets and extra tills don't go quiet: they talk to your main till across your own shop network. Staff Ordering tablets still send orders, tables can still be seated, moved and merged, and the waitlist keeps running. All you need is the main till switched on and everyone on the same Wi-Fi. (If a tablet can't reach the main till, see the "Not sent to the kitchen" pop-up in 13.1.)

What needs the internet

What still needs the internet. Three things wait for the connection to come back: sending texts (the waitlist "Notify" message and booking confirmations), making or editing a reservation (so two people can't book the same slot from outside the shop), and card payments. Everything else carries on.
Lists don't go blank. If a page can't load, BrewDesk shows the last copy it received rather than an empty screen — you'll see "Showing the last list received" at the top. It refreshes on its own when you're back online.
