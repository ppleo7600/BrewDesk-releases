22Multiple devices & reliability

You can use more than one device at the same time. If one breaks, another one keeps your shop open. Let's set up your devices.

  1. Sign in with the owner's email on the devices that will be your main till and extra tills. Staff Ordering tablets, customer displays and waitlist kiosks don't use the owner's email sign-in — you approve them at the main till (Add a device to your main till).
  2. Give each device a job. Pick main till (your number-one device), extra till (a helper device), Staff Ordering, customer display (a screen for customers), or waitlist kiosk.
  3. Need to change a device's job later? Ask for the change on that device, then approve it at the main till (Change a device's job & check the link).
  4. You pick one device as your main till — it's your anchor. It does not hand off on its own. If it ever goes offline, BrewDesk alerts you at once so you can turn it back on or make another device the main till.
  5. See Device modes & customer display to set a device's mode, including Staff Ordering.
If your main till goes offline. You get told right away — a note in your inbox, an email, and a banner on your other tills. Your other tills keep working, but QR and online orders pause until the main till is back (see QR code ordering). To make a different device the main till, you switch it yourself and BrewDesk emails you a code to confirm — nothing switches over silently.
Give each device a name. In Settings › Devices, tap a device's name (or the little pencil next to it), type something like "Counter 1" or "Bar iPad", and save. You'll see "Device name updated." The name shows on every device, so you always know which tablet is which. Your tills that connect only over the shop network can't be renamed.
Taking the main till off the list? If QR or online ordering is on, BrewDesk warns you first: "This is your Main POS. QR and online ordering are on — while no Main POS is connected, new customer orders can't be taken or printed in the kitchen." It only warns — you can still go ahead. If you can, make another device the main till first.
Show this off

Your till never dies. Spill a drink on it? Grab any laptop or tablet and you're back in minutes. No special machines, and you're never locked in.

Add a device to your main till

When you add a staff ordering tablet, a customer display, or a waitlist kiosk (self check-in) screen, your main till finds it for you. There is no code to type and no setting to hunt for. It even works when the shop has no internet.

What you need

The new device must have the BrewDesk app installed (iPad or desktop; the Android app is not available yet), and it must be on the same shop Wi-Fi as your main till. A page opened in a web browser can't be registered as a device — you'll see "Add devices from the BrewDesk app" instead.

  1. Open the BrewDesk app on the new device. On the sign-in screen, tap "Register as a new device at Main POS." If the device has no internet, it goes to this screen on its own.
  2. You'll see "Looking for Main POS..." and then the request is sent automatically. There is nothing more to tap on the new device — just leave it on "Waiting for approval at the Main POS."
  3. A popup called New device pairing request appears on your main till by itself. It shows which device asked, with its name and address.
  4. Under Device role, pick what this device is for — Staff Ordering, Customer Display, or Waitlist Kiosk (self check-in). The approve button stays off until you pick one.
  5. Tap Approve. If you don't recognise the device, tap Deny.
  6. The new device shows New device registration complete and goes straight into the job you picked for it.
You choose the job, not the new device. A new device can't tell your till "I'm a staff ordering tablet." Only the person standing at the main till picks the job — so a tablet someone quietly plugs in can't decide for itself what it's allowed to do.
This never makes a main till. Your main till is always set up by signing in with your email, and BrewDesk emails you a code to confirm when you change it. Extra tills (Sub POS) are also registered by signing in with email and confirming a code.
Says "Couldn't find the Main POS"? Usually the two devices are on different Wi-Fi. Connect to the shop Wi-Fi — not guest Wi-Fi and not a phone hotspot — then tap Try again. If the popup sits too long you'll see Request expired; just start again from the new device.

On the main till, Settings › Devices now shows a How to add a device guide instead of register buttons — registering always starts on the new device.

Change a device's job & check the link

You can change what an already-paired device does, or check whether it's still talking to your main till.

  1. On a Staff Ordering device, tap the device mode icon ("Switch device mode") at the top of the screen.
  2. Change device role opens. You don't need a code again — this device is already registered.
  3. Pick the new job — Staff Ordering (take orders from any device), Customer Display (show orders to customers), or Waitlist Kiosk (self check-in for the waitlist).
  4. Nothing changes on the tablet yet. It says "Waiting for Main POS approval" — "Ask the owner to approve this change on the Main POS screen. Nothing has changed on this device yet."
  5. Go to your main till. A card pops up by itself, tagged "Role change — existing device": "This device is already registered and is asking to change its role. Approving changes what it does; its login is not affected." Tap Approve — or Deny if you didn't expect it. The tablet then switches.
  6. At the top of that window you'll see the link status. Connected to the main POS means all is well. No main POS connected means it hasn't found it yet — tap Check again.
  7. To remove this device completely, tap Log out & unregister at the bottom. It returns to the sign-in screen, and after signing back in you can choose its job from scratch.
When something looks wrong — Connection log. Tap the connection log icon at the top of the screen to see, on one page, which shop and job this device is registered to, how long it has been linked to the main till, and how many orders haven't been sent up yet. When you contact support, Copy all and paste it in — that's the fastest way to get help.
Changing a device's job now needs your approval. Whoever is holding the tablet can ask, but only the main till can say yes — so a guest or a passer-by can't quietly turn your kiosk into an ordering device. The request expires after 2 minutes ("This request expires in {seconds}s — approve now or ask the device to send a new one"); if it runs out, just ask again from the tablet.
Turning a device into a Sub POS asks twice. A Sub POS can take money, so the main till warns you first — "This device will be able to take payments." / "A Sub POS can ring up orders, take payment and issue refunds. Approve only a device you set up yourself." Tap Continue to confirm. If you don't recognise the device, tap Deny.

When you hit the device limit

Your plan includes a certain number of tills and customer displays. When you reach that number, BrewDesk doesn't just refuse — it offers you a straight swap of one device for another.

Where to find it

Settings › Devices shows a warning strip at the top as soon as you reach the limit (not only when you go over it), counting what you're using — for example POS 1/1 and Customer display 1/1 on Pro, or POS 3/3 and Customer display 3/3 on Business. Each device also shows Last seen, so you can tell which one has been sitting in a drawer.

  1. Register the new till as usual. If you're at the limit you'll see "You've reached your plan's device limit. Replace an existing device, or upgrade your plan to add more."
  2. A Replace a device picker opens, listing the devices you could retire. Pick one.
  3. That's it — the old device is disconnected and the new one takes its place on the spot.
  4. Changed nothing on that list for a while? If the device you picked was already disconnected you'll see "That device was already disconnected, or this list is out of date — refreshing the list." Pick again from the fresh list.
Your main till is never in that list. Replacing the main till is a bigger deal, so it has its own route: the Replace Main POS card, which confirms and emails you a code first. The quick swap above is only for the extra devices.
We email you too. On the web, when a device is turned away for hitting the limit, BrewDesk emails the store owner as well — the message says "We've also emailed the store owner about this." So if a member of staff hits this at 7am, you find out without them having to explain it.
Staff Ordering has its own count. Pro includes 3 Staff Ordering slots (Business has no limit). Run out of slots and the registration screen tells you plainly: "All 3 Staff Ordering slots are in use (3/3). Release a device in Settings → Devices to add another." If your plan doesn't include it at all: "Staff Ordering isn't included in your current plan."

On an iPad or the desktop app the wording is slightly shorter and points you to your BrewDesk account on the web to change your plan — the swap itself works exactly the same.
