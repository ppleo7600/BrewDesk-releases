17Customers & loyalty

This part helps your customers come back. BrewDesk builds a list of your customers all by itself, and you can reward people for visiting again.

Where to turn it on

Turn on loyalty stamps in Settings › Add-ons › Loyalty / Stamps (a Pro add-on); it needs CRM/Customers on first (tap Enable Both if asked). For the line-up, turn on Settings › Add-ons › Waitlist (needs Tables on first).

  1. Just take orders as normal. BrewDesk quietly builds a customer list from those orders.
  1. Tap a customer's name any time to see how often they have visited.
  2. Want to reward regulars? Turn on loyalty stamps. This gives each customer a digital stamp card (like a coffee card on their phone).
  3. Customers earn a stamp each visit. After enough stamps, they get a reward, like a free drink.
  4. Got a queue? Use the waitlist to hold the line, then send a text when a table is ready.
Show this off

Your customer list grows on its own. No paper, no typing names in. Every order quietly adds to your regulars list and their visit history.

Loyalty stamps

How to set it up and earn. A stamp card on the phone. Each visit earns a stamp; enough stamps win a reward. Here's how to set it up and use it.

  1. Set it up. Open Settings › Add-ons, turn the Loyalty / Stamps card ON, then tap the card's Settings button. The Loyalty screen opens with two tabs: Overview and Settings. Set the stamp count and reward in the Settings tab.
  2. Choose how many stamps earn a reward (Stamps Required, like 10).
  3. Pick the reward — a Free Item or a "% Discount" — and give it a name.
  4. Choose how long the reward lasts, then tap Save.
  5. How a customer earns a stamp: when you take their order, tap Link Customer and pick them (or add them). When the order is paid, they get a stamp.
  6. Redeem a reward: once they have enough stamps, a reward shows on their next order — tap Use Reward to take it off the bill.
Turn it on first: loyalty needs the CRM/Customers add-on on first (this is noted in the box above — tap Enable Both if asked).
