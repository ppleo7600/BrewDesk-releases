4Building your menu

Your menu is the list of things you sell. It fills up the order screen. Let's add your items and sort them into groups.

Categories & items

  1. Tap Manage › Menu Items in the left sidebar. Top-right you'll see the buttons "AI Fill", "Export", "Import", "Move" and "Add Item"; "Add Category" sits at the far left of the category tab row.
  2. Make a category first. Tap "Add Category", type a name like "Coffee", and save. (You need at least one category before "Add Item" works.)
  3. Tap "Add Item". A side panel opens. Fill in: Item Name, Description (optional), Category, Price, and Stock Status (the "In Stock" toggle).
  4. Add a photo. Tap "Upload" to use your own picture, or "AI Generate" to make one.
  5. Tap "Save". The item shows up in its category.
What is a category? It is just a group, like a folder. It keeps your menu tidy so items are easy to find.

Option groups & presets

  1. Want to give choices? Attach option groups (like milk or size) in the item panel. You build groups with "Add Group", pick a type (Single / Multi / Quantity), and add choices with prices.
Ready-made option groups. Don't want to build groups from scratch? In the item panel tap "Add from Presets" to open the Preset Library — ready-made groups like Milk, Size, Temperature, and Sweetness for cafes, or Spice, Protein, and Sides for restaurants. Tap a card to add the whole group to your menu; it shows an "Added" badge once it's in.

AI photos & AI Fill

  1. Want a nicer photo? In the item panel, pick a background style (Light / Dark / Scene), then tap "AI Generate". It makes 3 photos — tap the one you like.
Do the whole menu at once with "AI Fill". Tap "AI Fill", pick which items (All items / Missing image only / Specific category), pick what to make (Image + Description / Image only / Description only), pick a background style (Light — Pure white studio / Dark — Dark charcoal / Scene — Café bokeh), then tap "Generate for N items". Review the results and tap "Apply".
Show this off

The AI can write your whole menu for you — and even make item photos. So you start with a full menu in seconds, not a blank screen.

Import & export

  1. Already have a menu in a spreadsheet? Tap "Import" to bring it all in at once. Tap "Export" to download your menu as a spreadsheet.
