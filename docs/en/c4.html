4Building your menu

Your menu is the list of things you sell. It fills up the order screen. Let's add your items and sort them into groups.

Categories & items

  1. Tap Manage › Menu Items in the left sidebar. Top-right you'll see the buttons AI Fill, Export, Import, "Move" and Add Item; Add Category sits at the far left of the category tab row.
  1. Make a category first. Tap Add Category, type a name like "Coffee", and save. (You need at least one category before Add Item works.)
  1. Tap Add Item. A side panel opens. Fill in: Item Name, Description (optional), Category, Price, and Stock Status (the "In Stock" toggle).
  1. Add a photo. Tap Upload to use your own picture, or AI Generate to make one.
  2. Tap Save. The item shows up in its category.
What is a category? It is just a group, like a folder. It keeps your menu tidy so items are easy to find.
Minimum quantity on the first order. Enter 2–99 in the Min. quantity on first order field in the item panel, and that item must be ordered at least that many times the first time it is added to an order. Leave it empty for no limit. Below the minimum, the POS will not send the order to the kitchen, and a guest ordering by QR cannot lower the quantity under it.

Sale hours

For items you only sell at certain times — a breakfast menu, a happy hour — set Sale hours and they hide themselves outside those times.

Where to find it

The Sale hours field in the category or item editor. The choices are Always on sale and Set hours; an item can also choose Follow category or Set for this item.

  1. Pick Set hours, then the days and a Start and End time. Add time range lets you add more than one range.
  2. If the end is earlier than the start, it runs until that time the next day (for example 22:00 to 02:00).
  3. Save. An item left on Follow category simply uses its category's hours.
What does it look like outside the hours? Guests and staff order devices don't see it, and the main POS shows it dimmed as Not on sale now. If you really must sell it at the till, tap it and in the "Not on sale right now" window choose Sell this once to sell it on that order only. A staff PIN can't approve this; it takes a manager or owner PIN.
Set your store time zone first. Sale hours follow the store time zone, and they don't apply until it's set.

Add options right from the menu item

  1. Want to give choices? Attach option groups (like milk or size) in the item panel. You build groups with Add Group, pick a type (Single / Multi / Quantity), and add choices with prices.
Ready-made option groups. Don't want to build groups from scratch? In the item panel tap Add from Presets to open the Preset Library — ready-made groups like Milk, Size, Temperature, and Sweetness for cafes, or Spice, Protein, and Sides for restaurants. Tap a card to add the whole group to your menu; it shows an Added badge once it's in.

AI photos & AI Fill

  1. Want a nicer photo? In the item panel, pick a background style (Light / Dark / Scene), then tap AI Generate. It makes 3 photos — tap the one you like.
Do the whole menu at once with "AI Fill". Tap AI Fill, pick which items (All items / Missing image only / Specific category), pick what to make (Image + Description / Image only / Description only), pick a background style (Light — Pure white studio / Dark — Dark charcoal / Scene — Café bokeh), then tap Generate for N items. Review the results and tap Apply.
Show this off

The AI can write your whole menu for you — and even make item photos. So you start with a full menu in seconds, not a blank screen.

Import & export

  1. Already have a menu in a spreadsheet? Tap Import to bring it all in at once. Tap Export to download your menu as a spreadsheet.
