21Your plan & billing

A plan is the level of BrewDesk you use. You can start for free and move up later. You manage it all in one place.

  1. You can stay on the Starter plan, which is free.
  2. Or you can try Pro or Business free for one month. You don't need a credit card to start the trial.
  3. When you decide to keep going, add a way to pay (like a card).
  4. To check your plan, your payment card, or your bills, go to Billing.
  5. The price is set to suit the country you are in.
A free trial means you use the full plan for a month at no cost. If you don't want to keep it, you simply don't add a card.

Deleting your account

Closing the shop, or moving on from BrewDesk? You can delete your account. Nothing disappears the moment you tap it — it runs 14 days later, and you can undo it any time before that.

Where to find it

Open your account settings from the account menu at the top right, then the "Delete Account" tab. Only the owner can do this.

  1. Read the list of what goes — store details, menu, order and payment history, subscription and invoices, closing reports, all of it.
  2. If you might need any of it later, tap "Export data" and save a copy first. Once it runs, there's no way to get it back.
  3. Type DELETE in capital letters in the confirm box.
  4. Tap "Send verification code". A 6-digit code goes to the owner's email.
  5. Enter the code from the email. The button then shows the real date (for example "Delete account on August 14, 2026") — tap it, and you're booked in and signed out.
Changed your mind? Sign back in and you'll see a banner across the top plus a "Deletion D-N" badge counting down. Just tap "Cancel deletion" and it's off — no code needed this time. The owner and managers can cancel it.
Your shop keeps running for those 14 days. Orders and payments work exactly as normal. Subscription billing is paused, and you can't switch plans while the countdown is on.
Why the email code? So nobody can wipe out your shop by accident — or on purpose. Only someone who can open the owner's inbox can schedule it.

Good to know: we keep a separate record of deletion requests and cancellations, so if there's ever an "I didn't delete that" moment, it can be checked.

Change your login email

If you mistyped your email when you signed up, the verification mail is going somewhere you can't reach — and resending it just sends it to the same wrong place again. Now you can correct the address yourself.

Where to find it

Account menu (top right) → Account Settings → Profile tab → "Wrong email address? Change it". Owner account only.

  1. Tap "Wrong email address? Change it". The form shows your current address so you can see what's wrong.
  2. Type the New email address — the one you can actually receive mail at.
  3. Type your Current password. This is required: your email is also how you sign in, so BrewDesk checks it's really you.
  4. Tap "Change email & send link". You'll see "Saved. A verification link is on its way to … — open it to unlock BrewDesk."
  5. Open your mail and tap the link. Done.
Nothing locks you out at day seven any more. The old "verify or you're locked" popup is gone. Instead there's a quiet banner with a Resend button — and now, next to it, a way to fix the address itself. Resending to a typo was never going to work.
If the address is already taken by another BrewDesk account, you'll be told plainly rather than left wondering. Same for a wrong password.

Installer partner

Some shops are set up by a local installer or dealer, who keeps a hand on the menu and the settings for you. If that's you, you can see exactly who has that access — and end it yourself, whenever you like.

Where to find it

Account menu (top right) → "Installer partner". Owner account only, and the menu item only appears when there is an approved connection — no menu for shops that don't use an installer, or that only have a request waiting.

  1. Open it. You'll see who is connected and since when — "Connected since 3 Feb 2026" — and if another installer has asked and is still waiting, "Awaiting your approval" with the date they asked is shown too.
  2. To end it, tap "Disconnect" next to their name.
  3. Confirm: "This installer will lose access to this store immediately. You can approve them again later if you change your mind."
The decision is yours, not theirs. Only the store owner can disconnect an installer — an installer can't quietly remove the record of their own access. A request that is still waiting is handled in the approval email the installer's request sends to the owner, not in this menu — that email has both an approve and a decline link.

While an installer is working in your shop on your behalf, their name is shown on their screen and recorded next to what they did in your activity log — so "who changed this price?" always has an answer.
