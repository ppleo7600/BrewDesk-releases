8Discounts & promotions

A discount takes money off a sale. You can set up happy hours and deals once, and BrewDesk does the math for you at the till.

  1. Open Discounts and tap "Add Discount". The "Create Discount" form opens.
  2. Type a Discount Name.
  3. Pick the Discount Type — "Percentage" (like 10% off) or "Fixed Amount" (like $2 off) — and type the Value.
  4. Set the Schedule: a start and end date, "All Day" or a time range, "No End Date" if it never stops, and Repetition (does not repeat / weekly / monthly / custom).
  5. Choose where it applies under Applicable Scope: "All" items, or tick specific categories or items with the checkboxes.
  6. Choose how it fires: Automatic (it comes off by itself at checkout) or Manual (staff tap "Apply Discount" at checkout and pick it).
  7. Optional: set a Minimum Order Value, and turn on "Allow Stacking" to let it combine with other discounts.
  8. Tap "Save". Your discount is ready.
Good to know: BrewDesk always works out the tax after the discount, so the total is always right.

Quick discount or "on the house" at the till

Set-up discounts are for things that happen every week. This is for the one-off — a regular you want to look after, a drink you remade, a coffee for a friend. You do it right in the cart, in about three taps.

Where to find it

In the cart: tap an item, then "Discount" or "Free" on that line. For the whole order, tap the "Disc" button next to Bill at the bottom.

  1. Tap "Discount" on one item, or "Disc" for the whole order. A keypad panel opens.
  2. Choose how you want to type it — Amount (money off) or % Percent.
  3. Type the number on the keypad.
  4. Tap a reason chip — Friend, Apology, Staff, Tasting, Mistake — or "+ New" to write your own. Your own reasons come back next time.
  5. Tap "Done". The cart total updates straight away.
  6. To take it off again, tap the same button — it now says "Remove Discount" (or "Remove Free").
Typed the full amount? BrewDesk asks "Full amount entered — record as Free instead?" and offers "Record as Free". Say yes — then your reports can tell "given away" apart from "discounted", which is what you actually want to know at the end of the month.
Made a discount in Settings and marked it "Manual"? It shows up as a tile inside this panel when you're adjusting the whole order, so staff just tap it instead of typing. If you haven't made any yet, there's a "Create discount" button that takes you to the right screen.
Changed the quantity after discounting? The adjustment is cleared and you'll see "Adjustment cleared — quantity changed". It's deliberate: 2,000 off two cups is not 2,000 off five cups, and BrewDesk would rather ask you again than quietly guess.

Free items are shown on the closing summary on their own line — see Chapter 17.
