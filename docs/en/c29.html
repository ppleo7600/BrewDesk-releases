14Owner app (check your store from your phone)

Away from the shop? Your phone can show today's sales, which tables are seated right now, your closing reports and your alerts. BrewDesk Owner isn't an App Store download — it's a web app you open in your phone's browser, and once you add it to your Home Screen it opens from an icon like any other app. There's no password: you sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email.

Where to find it

Open brewdesk.io/owner in your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android) — it's your till's address with /owner on the end. Sign in with the store owner's email, or with an email the owner has invited as an admin.

  1. Open brewdesk.io/owner. You'll see the BrewDesk logo and an email box.
  2. Type your store account's email under EMAIL ADDRESS and tap Send code.
  3. Find the 6-digit code in your inbox, enter it under VERIFICATION CODE and tap Verify.
  4. On your own phone, leave Keep me signed in on this device switched on — next time the app opens straight away with no code. On a shared device, switch it off.
  5. Once you're in: if you have one store, its dashboard opens; with several stores you land on a Home screen that adds them all up.
No code? Check your spam folder, wait a moment, then tap Resend code — it unlocks when the timer next to it runs out. Typed the wrong address? Tap Change email and enter it again.
Change the app's language. Tap the gear (Settings) at the top right → Language and pick one of the listed languages. Amounts are shown in your store's currency.

The owner app is mainly for keeping an eye on your store. Menu, prices, staff PINs and device settings are still changed on the till (POS).

Add it to your Home Screen

Add it once and you can open BrewDesk Owner from its icon instead of typing the address. No App Store needed.

  1. Android (Chrome): when the Add BrewDesk Owner to your Home Screen banner appears, tap Add.
  2. iPhone (Safari): follow the three steps on screen — tap Safari's Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add at the top right.
  3. Closed the banner? You can bring it back any time from the gear (Settings) → Add to Home Screen (PWA).
On iPhone, add it from Safari. Other browser apps may not offer Add to Home Screen, or may behave differently. If the icon on your Home Screen is named BrewDesk Owner, it was added correctly.

Home & store dashboard

Home gives you today at a glance. Pick a store to open its dashboard; the screen refreshes on its own every 15 seconds. Available for Pro Business stores.

Where to find it

Bottom tab Home. With more than one store, tap the store-name box near the top to switch between All stores and each store.

  1. Several stores: Home shows Today · All stores total sales, the change against the same time yesterday, orders, Avg ticket and how many stores are open. Tap a store in the Stores list to open its dashboard.
  2. At the top of the dashboard, choose a period: Today · Yesterday · 7D · 30D. The change against the previous period sits next to the sales figure.
  3. Switch the chart between Hourly and Daily.
  4. Scroll down for Orders · Avg ticket, Payments (cancellations, refunds and tips), Payment mix (card vs cash), Top Sellers and Peak hours (how busy each day and hour has been over the last 30 days).
Seeing "Store POS offline"? Your store's main till isn't connected to the internet. The shop can keep trading (see Working offline), but those sales haven't come up yet, so the app shows less than you've really taken. Sales after the last synced time fill in once the till reconnects. If sales look like zero, check for this badge before you worry.
A store that's blurred and locked in the list is on the Starter plan. Tapping it shows Available on the Pro plan. You can change plans in the bottom Billing tab (Billing, plans & message top-ups).

The official numbers for a day are the ones in the closing report you run on the till. The dashboard shows the day while it's still going — see Reports & closing the day for how to close.

Live — what's happening right now

See seated tables, incoming online orders and today's reservations from anywhere — and handle a few of them straight from your phone.

Where to find it

Bottom tab Live. The small tabs at the top depend on the add-ons your store has on: Dine-in always shows, Online shows when QR/online ordering is on, and Reservations shows when reservations or the scheduler is on (see Add-ons & extras).

  1. Dine-in: how many tables and guests are in, plus how long each table has been seated and its running total. Tap a table to see what's on its order. Refreshes every 10 seconds.
  2. Online: pickup, delivery and QR orders, split into New and Preparing. Tap Accept on a new order, and Mark picked up once the customer has collected it.
  3. Reservations: move between days with the date bar, or open the calendar to jump to a date. Confirmed bookings have Seat · No-show · Cancel buttons.
Cancelling tells the guest. Tapping Cancel asks Cancel this reservation? first, because the guest is notified. For running bookings see Reservations & Scheduler; for QR orders see QR code ordering.

Reports & AI analysis

Once you close the day on the till, the closing report is on your phone too — along with an AI write-up of how the day went.

Where to find it

Bottom tabs Reports and AI. With more than one store, pick the store in the box at the top first.

  1. Daily: pick a date to open that day's closing report — total and net sales, tax, tips, discounts, orders and average ticket, cancellations and refunds, amounts by payment method, sales by category, and who closed it (Closed by).
  2. On the same screen, Cash drawer reconciliation shows the opening float, what was expected, what was counted and the difference: Balanced, Over or Short. See Cash drawer.
  3. Period: set From and To to see the total and a day-by-day list for the closed days in between.
  4. Z Report: the same content as the Z report your till prints, by date.
  5. Tap Download (CSV) at the bottom to save the report you're looking at as a CSV file that opens in Excel.
  6. The AI tab opens on your most recent closed day: Summary, Highlights, Recommendations and Alerts. Change the date to read earlier analyses.
"No closing report for this date yet" means the day hasn't been closed on the till. Reports and AI analysis are both built from closed days. For a day you forgot to close, see Late orders & missed days.

AI analysis is part of the AI Smart Insights included with Pro and above. To export many days at once, use Export your sales on the till.

Alerts & email links

Things you need to know — a failed payment, a subscription change, a refund, a day close on hold, a new AI insight — collect in the app's alert inbox. And the buttons in BrewDesk's report and alert emails open the matching screen in the owner app.

Where to find it

The bell icon (Alerts) at the top right. A number on it means unread alerts.

  1. Switch between All and Unread.
  2. Narrow by period (Today · Last 7 days · Last 30 days) and by store (All stores or one store).
  3. Tap an alert to mark it read; the number on the bell goes down.
There are no phone push notifications. You check alerts in the inbox when you open the app. Urgent things (main till offline, a failed payment and so on) still go to your email as well.
Screens opened from an email button are read-only. Buttons in emails such as the daily report, a subscription change, main till offline or a support reply open that store's screen without signing in, with a Read-only view. strip at the top. You can look but not change anything — to make changes, tap Sign in to make changes on the strip and sign in with a code. Email links expire after a while; when one does, just sign in to the app directly.

An email link works like a key to your store's screens. Please don't forward those emails to anyone.

Billing, plans & message top-ups

Change your plan, manage your saved card, top up SMS and WhatsApp messages and check recent payments — all from your phone. Store owners only: signed in as a manager, this tab doesn't appear.

Where to find it

Bottom tab Billing. With several stores you first see billing for all of them; tap a store's Manage to open that store. With one store it opens straight away.

  1. Under Subscription, check your current plan and its renewal date. Tap a plan's Details to see what's included.
  2. To change plan, tap Upgrade to … or Switch to … on the plan you want. A secure payment page (Stripe) opens; finish there.
  3. Payment methods lists your saved card. Cards are saved automatically when you subscribe or top up, so there's nothing to add here — you can only Remove a card you no longer need.
  4. Under Messages, see how many SMS and WhatsApp messages are left and how many you've used this month, then tap Top up. You choose the amount on the payment page, and you can switch on Auto top-up to refill automatically when the balance runs low.
  5. Recent payments lists your past charges.
If a reseller (installer partner) handles your billing, you'll see a Contact your reseller card instead of plan cards and top-up buttons. Ask them to renew or top up. To pay BrewDesk yourself instead, disconnect them from Manage partners (see Admins, installer partners & support).

For the differences between plans and the free trial, see Your plan & billing; for using messages, see Messaging your customers.

Admins, installer partners & support

Without going into the shop, you can give a manager access to the owner app, cut off an installer partner, or contact support.

Where to find it

The gear (Settings) at the top right → under Store: Manage store (admins & permissions) and Installer partners. These two rows only appear for that store's owner.

  1. Tap Add Admin, fill in Name, Email and Role, then tap Send Invite. The role is Manager (can view the admin list and activity history) or Owner (can also add and remove admins).
  2. When the invited person signs in to brewdesk.io/owner with that email, their status changes from Invite pending to Active.
  3. To take someone off, tap Remove next to their name and confirm. You can't remove yourself, or the store's last owner.
  4. Activity Log shows admin invites and removals, role changes, owner-app sign-ins, till sign-ins and PIN creation or changes.
  5. Installer partners shows which partner is connected and when they last accessed your store. Disconnect cuts their access; a sheet first explains what will happen, and Keep connected backs out.
  6. For help, open Support · My tickets and tap New inquiry. Choose the store and type (bug, feature request or other), add a subject and message, and send. Replies appear on the same screen so you can keep the conversation going. It's the same ticket list as support on the till.
Till PINs are separate. Adding an admin here only lets them into the owner app. To sign in on the till, they still need a staff PIN made on the till — see Staff & permissions.
Managers see less. A manager can see sales, Live, reports, AI and alerts, but not the Billing tab or the store-management and installer-partner rows. Roles are set per store, so one person can be an owner at store A and a manager at store B.

For what an installer connection means, see Installer partner. When you're done, Sign out is at the bottom of Settings.
