27FAQ

The questions staff, owners and installers ask most often. The answers are kept short — follow the link at the end of each one for the full steps.

Getting started, plans & account

  1. Do I need a credit card or special hardware to start? No. You sign up with an email and password, and BrewDesk runs on the laptop or tablet you already have. The Pro and Business free trials don't need a credit card to start either. → Quick start · Welcome & overview
  2. How do I clear out the orders I rang up while practising? When you go live, choose Reset and go live (wipe the practice records and start clean). Only the practice orders, sales and closing records are removed — your menu, tax, printers, staff and Stripe settings all stay. → Choose how to start
  3. How do I change plan, or stop after the free trial? Your plan, payment card and invoices are all under Billing. If you don't want to carry on after the trial, simply don't add a card — you can keep using the free Starter plan. In the iPad and desktop apps, change your plan from your BrewDesk account on the web. → Your plan & billing
  4. I asked to delete my account — can I take it back? Yes. Deletion only happens after 14 days. Until then, sign in again and tap Cancel deletion at the top of the screen and it's as if you never asked. No code is needed, and the owner or a manager can cancel it. → Deleting your account
  5. How do I turn on extras like QR ordering or Staff? Go to Settings › Add-ons and tap the switch on the card. Free add-ons work on every plan; some (Online Ordering/QR, Loyalty, Staff, Scheduler and others) come with the Pro plan; text messages (SMS) are pay-as-you-go. → Turn add-ons on and off

Payments, refunds & closing

  1. What are the card fees? BrewDesk adds no fee to your card sales, on every plan. The money goes straight into your own Stripe account; the only fee is Stripe's standard fee, which you pay to Stripe directly. Linking Stripe is free on every plan. → Getting paid
  2. The Card button is grey and won't tap. You haven't linked your Stripe account yet. Connect it in Settings › Payments. Cards taken on a separate terminal can still be recorded with the card-brand buttons or Card (External). → Getting paid
  3. Can staff give refunds? A refund only goes through once a manager approves it with their PIN, so a staff member can't do it alone. The owner decides which roles may refund in Settings › Staff Permissions. → Refunds · Permissions, manager PIN & role chip
  4. I forgot to close the day. Next time you open BrewDesk, an Unclosed Business Days window lists the missed dates, and new sales pause until they're closed. The easy choice is Auto-Close All; to check each day yourself, choose Close One by One. → Late orders & missed days
  5. A payment came in after I closed the day. The payment goes through as normal, then you'll see Additional Closing Needed. Review and close, and BrewDesk saves it as a separate additional closing report — the original Z-Report never changes. → Late orders & missed days
  6. The Close Day button is greyed out. A cash drawer session is still open, or there are unpaid orders left. Use Open drawer next to the message to count the cash and end the session, or settle or cancel the open orders, then close again. → Cash drawer

Devices, internet & printers

  1. What happens if the internet goes down? Keep taking orders, cash payments and printing receipts as usual. Orders are saved on the device and upload by themselves when the internet returns — never twice. Only connected card-reader payments, sending messages, and creating or changing bookings have to wait. → Working offline · What needs the internet
  2. Receipts or kitchen tickets aren't printing. Check the printer is on; failed prints wait in a list, so just tap Retry. Some Wi-Fi printers show No print confirmation and can't report a failure, so check the paper with your eyes. Need another copy? Open the order and tap Reprint. → Receipts & printers
  3. How many devices can I connect? It depends on the plan. Tills (including the main one): Starter and Pro 1, Business 3. Customer displays: Pro 1, Business 3. Staff ordering devices: Pro 3, Business unlimited. When you hit the limit, BrewDesk offers Replace a device to swap one out. → When you hit the device limit
  4. QR orders aren't reaching the till. QR orders come in through the main till, so if it's off or offline, new QR orders pause. While the internet is down, don't issue new table QR codes — take orders by hand instead. And in practice mode, guests can't place real QR orders at all. → Running it day to day · Going live
  5. If the main till goes off, does the shop stop? No. The other tills keep working, and BrewDesk tells you straight away — inbox alert, email and a banner on the other tills. QR and online orders do pause until the main till is back, and making another device the main till is something the owner does by hand, with an email code. → Multiple devices & reliability

Staff, PINs & data

  1. A staff member forgot their PIN. Open that person in Settings › Staff Permissions › Staff and tap "Reset to 0000". The owner or a manager can do it. Nobody can sign in with 0000, so have them set a new PIN straight away. → Forgot a PIN?
  2. I forgot the owner PIN or the manager PIN. For the owner PIN, tap "Forgot your PIN?" in the role chip panel and set a new one with the 6-digit code sent to your sign-up email. For the manager PIN, the owner simply sets a new one on the manager PIN card in Settings › Staff Permissions. → Forgot a PIN?
  3. How do I back up my store's data? On the backup page in Settings, tap Connect Google Drive to link your own Drive; from then on BrewDesk keeps saving new backups by itself. To get an older copy, tap Restore and pick one. For files to hand your accountant, use Accounting Export in Closing History. → Backups · Export your sales
