2Quick start — up and running in 5 minutes

This is the quickest way to make your very first sale. You can do it in about five minutes. Just follow each tiny step.

  1. Open a web browser and go to brewdesk.io.
  2. Type your email and a password to sign up. You do not need a credit card.
  3. Pick what kind of shop you have. Choose cafe, restaurant, bar, salon, spa, or bakery. BrewDesk sets things up to match.
  4. Tap the AI menu builder button. This makes a starter menu for you in seconds. (Or you can type a few items yourself.)
  5. Look for the order screen. Tap an item to add it to the order.
  6. Tap Cash to finish the sale right away. A "Sale complete" message appears. That's it — your first sale is done!
  7. Later, when you want to take cards, you can link your own Stripe account. (We show you how in Chapter 7.)
Don't worry if you get something wrong. You can change anything later in Settings. Nothing here is final.

First-time setup (the 7 steps)

When you first sign up, a friendly wizard walks you through a few simple steps. Here is what it asks, in order. Just tap your way through.

  1. Business Type — pick what kind of shop you have: cafe, restaurant, bar, salon, spa, or bakery.
  2. Store Info — type your store name, pick your country or region, and say how many registers (tills) you have.
  3. Service Mode — choose Counter (people order at the counter) or Table service (people order at their table).
  4. Add-ons — switch on the extra features you want. You can change these later, so don't worry.
  5. Plan — pick Starter (free), Pro, or Business. Pro and Business come with a 1-month free trial, and you don't need a credit card to start.
  6. Menu — start from a ready-made template, let the AI build it for you, or add items yourself. (More about the AI menu builder in Chapter 4.)
  7. Owner PIN — set a PIN to lock the screen. This step is optional.

That's it! You'll just confirm a quick tax note, and you're ready to sell.

The whole thing takes about 5 minutes. And remember — you can change anything later in Settings.

From practice mode to real trading (going live)

You start in practice mode. Tap anything, ring up pretend orders — none of it counts as real takings. When you're ready to actually trade, you switch to live.

Where to find it

While you're in practice mode there's a strip across the top of the screen: "Practice mode — sales are not counted as real revenue". Tap "Go Live" next to it.

  1. If anything's still missing, a checklist appears first — store name, country, currency, timezone, business type, confirmed tax rate, tax ID, store address, and at least one menu item.
  2. Tap "Set up" on a line to jump straight to that setting. When you've filled them in, tap "Re-check".
  3. Then pick one of three — "Still practicing" (carry on practising, nothing changes), "Reset and go live" (wipe the practice records and start clean), or "Already in business" (keep everything you've rung up so far, and tell us which day you actually opened).
  4. You get one more confirmation screen listing exactly what gets deleted and what stays.
  5. For the two real options BrewDesk emails you a code to type in — going live is a one-way door, so we check it's really you.
  6. Done. Your sales now count officially, and customers can order from their phones.
Already taken real money? Then "Still practicing" disappears. If BrewDesk can see real orders, real card payments or closed days on your account, that card is hidden and it says so: "Real sales detected — 'Keep practicing' is not available. Choose Reset (B) or Keep data & go live (C)." A panel shows you what it found — how many orders, how much, whether cards were used. Pretending yesterday's real takings were practice is how books stop balancing.
Do it on the main till. Going live can only be done from your main register: "Only the main POS can switch the store to live. Open this on your main register."
Why block it? If you start trading with your tax rate or timezone still blank, your first day's takings get recorded wrong — and that's much more painful to fix afterwards. So we ask you to confirm it once, up front.
Your menu, tax, printers, staff and Stripe settings all stay. Even if you choose "Reset & start", the only things removed are the practice orders, sales and closing records.

Good to know: while you're in practice mode, customers can't place real QR or online orders. Their screen says "This store is in practice mode".
