User Manual & Feature Guide

Everything your cafe, restaurant, bar, or salon needs to start selling — and a few things you'll be glad you have.

★ Up and running in 5 minutes ★ 0% platform card fees ★ Works offline ★ Your POS never dies

START HERE Why merchants love BrewDesk

The big things first — these are what set BrewDesk apart. Look for the ★ gold highlights throughout this guide; they mark features worth showing off.

★ Set up in 5 minutes

Sign up, pick your business type, let AI build a starter menu, and take your first order — no installer, no training, no credit card.

★ 0% platform card fees — ever

BrewDesk never takes a cut of your card sales. You keep more of every sale.*

★ Works offline, always open

The internet drops, you keep selling. Orders save on the device and sync the moment you're back online.

★ Your POS never dies

Any laptop or tablet can become your register in minutes. A broken device never closes your shop.

★ Connect your Stripe account — free

Take cards by linking your own Stripe account. Connecting is free on every plan, and BrewDesk adds 0% on top of Stripe's rate.

★ One app, many businesses

Cafe, restaurant, bar, bakery, hair salon, spa — BrewDesk reshapes itself to fit how you actually work.

* You pay your payment processor's standard rate directly to them — BrewDesk adds 0% on top.
