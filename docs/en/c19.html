19Backups

A backup is a spare copy of all your shop's information. BrewDesk can save one to your own Google Drive, so your data is always safe.

  1. Go to Settings and find the backup page.
  2. Tap Connect Google Drive and sign in to your Google account.
  3. That's it. From now on, BrewDesk saves a fresh backup on its own, again and again.
  4. Ever need an old copy back? Tap Restore and pick a backup.
Google Drive is a free online place to keep files. The backup goes into your own Drive, so only you can see it.
