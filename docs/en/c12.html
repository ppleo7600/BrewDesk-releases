18Messaging your customers

You can send your customers a receipt or a quick note. You can pick the way they like best — email, WhatsApp, or text.

Where to turn it on

Manage all three channels — email, SMS and WhatsApp — in Settings › Messaging. WhatsApp is not an add-on; it is a channel on this screen. SMS and WhatsApp are pay-as-you-go — add a little credit and you only pay for what you send.

Turn on a channel & top up

On the Usage tab of Messaging you'll see three cards side by side: Email, SMS (Credit Balance) and WhatsApp (WA Credit). Each card shows the credit left, how many you sent this month, a Top Up button and an Auto Top-Up switch.

  1. Email is ready to go from day one. Use it to send receipts and daily reports.
  2. WhatsApp doesn't need the add-on store. Turn the channel on in the WhatsApp card and add some credit, and you can send receipts, rewards, and "your table is ready" notes. When you turn the channel on you get an email to finish setting it up, and until you top up the card says "Enabled · add credit to send".
  3. A plain text message (an SMS) works the same way. Top up on the SMS card and you only pay for the texts you send.
  4. When credit runs low, tap Top Up at the top of that card. If you'd rather not keep topping up, turn on Auto Top-Up at the bottom of the card and choose how low it can get before it adds more, and how much to add. (In the iPad and desktop apps the card shows a channel on/off switch instead of a payment link.)

Opt-in & templates

Customers always say yes first before you message them. If they ever reply STOP, BrewDesk stops texting them right away. You'll find the wording of each message in the Templates tab and what was sent in the Log tab.

Opt-in means the customer chooses to get your messages. They are always in control.
