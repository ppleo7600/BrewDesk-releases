12Messaging your customers

You can send your customers a receipt or a quick note. You can pick the way they like best — email, WhatsApp, or text.

Where to turn it on

Set up WhatsApp & SMS in Settings › Messaging. SMS is pay-as-you-go — add a little balance ("Top up SMS") and you only pay for what you send.

  1. Email is ready to go from day one. Use it to send receipts and daily reports.
  2. Want to use WhatsApp? Turn it on in the add-on store. Then you can send receipts, rewards, and "your table is ready" notes.
  3. Want to send a plain text message (an SMS)? Turn that on too. You only pay for the texts you send.
  4. Customers always say yes first before you message them. If they ever reply STOP, BrewDesk stops texting them right away.
Opt-in means the customer chooses to get your messages. They are always in control.
