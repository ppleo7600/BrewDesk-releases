10Refunds

A refund is when you give a customer their money back. BrewDesk makes this safe — a manager has to say yes first, so it can't be used the wrong way.

  1. Go to Manage › Payment History in the left sidebar.
  2. Tap the order you want to refund to open it.
  3. Tap the FAIR-REFUND button under the receipt on the right (it is shown in capitals).
  1. Choose full refund to give all the money back (works the same day). Or choose partial refund to give back only some items or some money.
  2. A manager types their PIN (a secret code) to say yes.
  1. The refund is done. BrewDesk writes it down and fixes your day's totals for you.
What is a PIN? It is a short secret code, like the one on a bank card. It stops a refund from happening by mistake.
A partial refund now prints a partial receipt. The refund slip lists only the items you gave money back for — and the totals underneath (subtotal, discount, service charge, tax) are now the refunded share too, not the original order's figures. So the customer can add the slip up and it comes to exactly what went back on their card.

Order status in Payment History

The status shown on order cards and in order details in Payment History now appears in plain words in your language. Before, some statuses showed their internal name. On Hold (Held) is an order set aside for a moment, Open is an order that isn't paid yet, and Merged is an order that was combined with another table's order.

Refund confirmation email (what the customer receives)

After a refund, BrewDesk automatically emails the customer a refund confirmation. It's only sent when the order is linked to a customer with an email address; a customer with no email gets nothing.

Where to switch it on or off

The email box of Refund Confirmation ("Sent when a full or partial refund is issued") in the Refunds section of Settings › Notifications. It is on by default.

  1. The subject is "Refund processed — store name", or "Partial refund processed — store name" for a partial refund.
  2. At the top of the message are a REFUND PROCESSED banner (PARTIAL REFUND PROCESSED for a partial one), the title "Your Refund Has Been Processed", and "A refund has been issued for your order at store name."
  3. Below are the rows Order (order number) · Order Date · Refund Type (Full Refund / Partial Refund) · Items (the refunded items, for a partial refund) · Original Total · Refund Amount · Reason. Original Total only appears when it applies.
  4. If a tip was not refunded, a Tip Retained row appears with "The tip of amount was paid to the staff who served you and is not included in this refund."
  5. It ends with "Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your payment provider."
The email goes out in your store's language and date format. The subject and wording follow the store language set in Settings › Store Info, and the order date uses your store's time zone and your country's date format. The customer's email has no BrewDesk support or email-preferences links and no promotional line — only the name of the store it came from. Receipt emails go out in your store language the same way, and their subtotal and discount lines were tidied so they add up.
