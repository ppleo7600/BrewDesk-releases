9Refunds

A refund is when you give a customer their money back. BrewDesk makes this safe — a manager has to say yes first, so it can't be used the wrong way.

  1. Go to Manage › Payment History in the left sidebar.
  2. Tap the order you want to refund to open it.
  3. Tap the FAIR-REFUND button under the receipt on the right (it is shown in capitals).
  1. Choose full refund to give all the money back (works the same day). Or choose partial refund to give back only some items or some money.
  2. A manager types their PIN (a secret code) to say yes.
  1. The refund is done. BrewDesk writes it down and fixes your day's totals for you.
What is a PIN? It is a short secret code, like the one on a bank card. It stops a refund from happening by mistake.
A partial refund now prints a partial receipt. The refund slip lists only the items you gave money back for — and the totals underneath (subtotal, discount, service charge, tax) are now the refunded share too, not the original order's figures. So the customer can add the slip up and it comes to exactly what went back on their card.
