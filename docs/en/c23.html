5Staff quick start

A one-page guide for anyone running the till for the first time. It follows a shift from clock-in to clock-out. Each step is kept short — tap the links when you want the full story.

Get these from the owner first

① A staff account in your name and a 4-digit PIN (Add a staff member) · ② whether this store takes orders at the counter or at tables · ③ which manager to call for refunds or bigger discounts. Keep your PIN to yourself — the activity log records actions under your name.

When you arrive

  1. Clock in — tap the coloured role chip in the top bar to open the Time Clock panel. Tap In next to your name and enter your 4-digit PIN. If "Take the POS?" appears, choose "Yes" when you are the one running this till. (Used in stores with the Staff add-on — Staff & permissions.)
  2. PIN sign-in & Idle Lock — in stores that use Idle Lock, a till left alone for a while switches to the Select Profile lock screen. Tap your name and enter your PIN to carry on. The default PIN 0000 can't be used to sign in, so if yours is still 0000, ask the owner for a new one (Permissions, manager PIN & role chip, Forgot a PIN?).

Serving customers

  1. Take the order — at the counter, tap items into the order and tap Pay Now. At tables, tap the guest's table on the table map first. If a guest steps away, park the order with Hold (Taking orders, Hold an order).
  2. Take payment — tap Cash and enter the amount handed over; the change is shown. Tap Card when a card reader is connected, or a card-brand button (Record Only) when the card was taken on a separate terminal. Don't close the payment screen while the reader is still approving a card. When several people share a bill, use Split (Getting paid, Split the bill).
  3. Quick discount or free item — tap an item in the cart for the Discount and Free buttons; for the whole order, tap Disc at the bottom. Enter an amount or %, pick a reason chip (Friend · Apology · Staff · Tasting · Mistake) and tap Done (Quick discount or free item).
  4. Who can refund? — out of the box, the owner and managers can refund; a staff member needs a manager (or the owner) to approve with a PIN. The owner may have changed this in the permissions table, so when a customer asks for a refund, call a manager. Refunds are done from Manage › Payment History by opening the order (Refunds, permissions).

When something goes wrong

  1. The internet drops — an orange bar appears at the top. Keep taking orders as usual: they are saved on the device and upload by themselves when the connection returns. Only connected card-reader payments, sending messages and new bookings have to wait for the internet (Working offline, What needs the internet).
  2. A staff-ordering tablet says "Not sent to the kitchen" — check the main till is on and on the same store Wi-Fi, then tap "Try again". Tap "Entered on Main POS" only if you actually typed the order in on the main till (Staff Ordering).

Before you leave

  1. Tidy up open orders — settle anything on Hold or still unpaid, or tell a manager about it. Unpaid orders block closing the day (Late orders & missed days).
  2. The cash drawer and closing the day belong to managers and the owner — the Cash Drawer menu only appears for them. If you were holding the drawer, count it and hand it over to a manager (Cash drawer, Reports & closing the day).
  3. Clock out — tap the role chip, tap Out next to your name in the Time Clock and enter your PIN. If "Who takes over?" appears, pick the next person on the till or Return to Owner.
Unfamiliar screen? Don't just tap through it. Most messages say exactly what to do next. If you are still stuck, call a manager or check Troubleshooting & support.
